Type: IBM Plex Sans Hebrew 400/600/700 (all Hebrew + UI), IBM Plex Mono 500 (numbers, levels,
labels, source captions), Frank Ruhl Libre 700 (beat-5 subtitle only). Self-hosted woff2 in assets/fonts.
Kinetic type: leading 0.95, 12-col grid, right-aligned. 16:9 margins 120/104/96, gutter 24.
9:16 safe box x 90–990, y 240–1600. Min text 34px (16:9) / 44px (9:16); captions 22/26.
UI: real HTML components, 8px grid, 6px radius, no lorem. Paper grain 2% on light scenes only.
Orange on text fails contrast on paper, so orange is always a fill with ink text (chip, CTA, meter).
