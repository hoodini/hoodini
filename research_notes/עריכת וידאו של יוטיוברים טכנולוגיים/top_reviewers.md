## Q1: Who do press, rankings and other creators cite as the best tech reviewers right now?

### Takeaway
There is no single authoritative 2026 ranking. The same core names come up across lists: MKBHD, Mrwhosetheboss, Unbox Therapy, Linus Tech Tips, JerryRigEverything, iJustine, Austin Evans, Dave2D, plus "cinematic" mid-size channels such as Jonathan Morrison and MrMobile. By subscribers, Unbox Therapy, Mrwhosetheboss and MKBHD lead with about 21-25M each. LTT is about 17M and runs on a ~120-person company.

## Q2: What editing and production signatures define each creator?

### Takeaway
- **MKBHD:** minimal, clean, polished. A ~$250K robotic motion-control arm ("Colossus") does signature product hero shots, inside a Kearny, NJ studio with a dedicated robot room. Videos have grown to 9-14 minutes because many people watch on TVs.
- **Mrwhosetheboss:** high-budget entertainment. Stunt builds, a house-tech series, and a codified graphic style (fonts and outlines in written style documents).
- **LTT:** high volume, multiple formats, personality-led hosting, backed by Labs data.
- **Dave2D:** extreme minimalism (solid colours, straight lines, custom logo animations, lo-fi music).
- **JerryRigEverything:** a repeatable stress-test sequence.
- **Morrison and MrMobile:** short-film cinematic pacing.

### Inferences
- Robotic motion control is the clearest hardware signature at the top tier. It is mostly associated with MKBHD, who keeps a dedicated robot room. It gives repeatable, precise hero product moves that a solo creator can't easily copy.
- Codified style guides (Mrwhosetheboss) and multiple editing teams suggest that the top channels scale their "look" through documentation, not one auteur editor.
- Minimalism shows up across MKBHD and Dave2D, while energy and spectacle define Mrwhosetheboss and LTT. That gives two clear stylistic poles for comparison.

## Q3: What have creators and their editors said in interviews, BTS videos and podcasts about process, tools and team size?

### Takeaway
MKBHD has spoken most openly in 2025:
- About 17 staff
- Videos turned around in 4-5 days
- An "octopus" delegation model where he keeps on-camera, writing and reviewing
- A Dec 2025 workflow podcast on scripting and team collaboration

LTT has ~120 staff and restructured after its 2023 accuracy controversy. I found no primary 2025-2026 source on which editing software (Premiere, Resolve, FCP) any of these teams uses.

### Cited Findings
**MKBHD team and process**
- Team size is about 17: "the team was just me for quite a while." Quote: "Everyone in the studio does something that I was once doing myself." — [Think with Google, Sept 2025](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/marques-brownlee-interview/)
- A YesPress profile says 18 full-time staff in 2025. That conflicts with 17, and YesPress is less authoritative — [YesPress](https://yespress.io/marques-brownlee)
- Octopus analogy: creators hand off the "arms" of the work. His "three hearts" are being on camera, writing, and reviewing tech — [Think with Google](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/marques-brownlee-interview/)
- Turnaround: "We'll make a video in four or five days, and they're kind of shocked." He was talking about brand partners — [Think with Google](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/marques-brownlee-interview/)
- 13 Dec 2025, "State of the Workflow" podcast with Myke Hurley: covers "shaping a product narrative and writing scripts to collaborating with his team," "deciding what becomes a video," and "managing the pressure of creating at massive scale." Show notes list TickTick, Superhuman, Arc and Dia. No editing NLE is named — [Substack](https://cgpgrey.substack.com/p/state-of-the-workflow-marques-brownlee)
- A 2024 Stratechery interview (older) says he separates planning days from shooting and editing days, with projects taking from one day to one week — [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2024/an-interview-with-marques-brownlee-mkbhd-about-being-a-youtube-star/). From the search summary only.
- By 2026 he has reportedly formalised quality control for long-form videos: auditing past content for accuracy and adding pre-publication review steps — [YesPress](https://yespress.io/marques-brownlee). Weak source, so this needs verification.
- Waveform's long-time producers Adam Molina and Ellis Rovin handle technical and audio engineering (early 2026) — [wowplus](https://wowplus.net/marques-brownlee-creator-business-reviews-waveform-team/), [Wikipedia Waveform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waveform_(podcast)). Secondary source.

  - [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VCRdoIkRkg)
  - [videohighlight](https://videohighlight.com/v/F_H9yQhB6ec)

### Inferences
- MKBHD's 4-5 day turnaround with ~17 people, and LTT's ~120 people across many channels, show very different throughput strategies:
  - **MKBHD:** small team, polished flagship.
  - **LTT:** media company with many formats.
- MKBHD keeping writing and on-camera work for himself suggests that his signature lies in the script and narrative arc. Visual polish (robot, editing) is delegated.

### Gaps

- No Colin & Samir 2025-2026 episode with these creators about editing was found in my searches. I did not search it specifically for lack of budget.
- No named lead editors for MKBHD (e.g., the studio's editors) or Mrwhosetheboss were confirmed.
- The full transcript of the Dec 2025 State of the Workflow episode was not available.

## Q4: Is there data linking their style to view performance or retention?

### Takeaway
I found almost no public retention data. The only style-performance link I could source is MKBHD's own statement that longer (9-14 min), higher-polish videos suit growing TV viewership.

### Cited Findings
- MKBHD's videos grew from 3-4 minutes to 9-14 minutes, which suits viewers watching on TVs — [Think with Google, Sept 2025](https://business.google.com/en-all/think/search-and-video/marques-brownlee-interview/)

- Social Blade video-level pages for Mrwhosetheboss exist but were not fetched — [Social Blade videos](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/mrwhosetheboss/videos)

### Inferences
- From the figures above: ShortCircuit averages about 650K views per video and TechLinked about 515K. That suggests LMG's lower-polish secondary formats still pull large per-video audiences.

### Gaps
- None of these creators has published audience-retention curves or style A/B test results that I could find.
- No third-party analysis links specific editing traits (robot shots, motion graphics, pacing) to retention.
